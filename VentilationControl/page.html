Manual ventilation cycle

Heating and temperature limits apply to manual cycles too.

Cold-point humidity uses the selected cold air sensor unless a wall sensor is configured. It is an estimate of risk at that point, not proof that every wall is safe.

Fixed room sensors

Each room starts with its lowest valid temperature sensor from the initial snapshot. The selection stays fixed. Use the matching temperature and humidity sensors to calculate moisture; the cold-point sensor assesses the colder part of the room.

Operation & limits

Connections & equipment

The air selector uses true for internal air and false for outside air. Verify that the exhaust and return paths work, the room flaps and fan respond correctly, and the cooling interlock blocks cooling before enabling live outputs. An active air-intake lock is respected.

Accept external handover only after reviewing the air selector and ventilation-active flag. It clears the pending record without sending equipment commands.

Configuration backup

Export includes the saved room selections and settings. Import disables control, automatic mode, live outputs and web access until reviewed in the Symcon console. The central Configuration Backup module can discover this module’s native exporter and validator.
